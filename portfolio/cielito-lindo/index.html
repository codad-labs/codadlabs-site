Cielito Lindo

Draw a constellation, give it a name and a story, and watch it take its place in a sky built one contribution at a time.

Chart a Constellation Explore the Sky

The piece

A 20 by 20 foot canopy of several hundred LEDs overhead, along with an aurora of chiffon dancing in the wind. Users can create a constellation using a joystick and board of LED buttons, record its story on a vintage payphone handset, and send it up to join the sky. Narrated stories play as each constellation twinkles in and out of view. You might even catch a meteor shower, or a shooting streaking across the sky.

Support the build

Donations fund LEDs, solar power, fabrication, and studio space. Donors above a set amount earn a star of their own in the sky.
