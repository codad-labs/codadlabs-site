Cielito Lindo

Draw a constellation, give it a name and a story, and watch it take its place in a sky built one contribution at a time.

Chart a Constellation Explore the Sky

The piece

PLACEHOLDER: A 20 by 20 foot canopy of 400 LEDs hangs overhead. You press out a pattern on a board of glowing arcade buttons, record its story on a vintage payphone handset, and send it up to join the sky. Narrated stories play as each constellation appears.

Support the build

PLACEHOLDER: Donations fund LEDs, solar power, and fabrication. Donors above a set amount earn a star of their own in the sky.
