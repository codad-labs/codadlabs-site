Cielito Lindo

Draw a constellation, give it a name and a story, and watch it take its place in a sky built one contribution at a time.


Can't make it to one of our events? You can still add a constellation, or view all the constellations that have been submitted so far. These constellations will be displayed in the sky at any event we bring Cielito to, and will live on in the sky eternally.
Chart a Constellation Explore the Sky

The piece

A 20 by 20 foot canopy of several hundred LEDs overhead, along with an aurora of chiffon dancing in the wind. Users can create a constellation using a joystick and board of LED buttons, record its story on a vintage payphone handset, and send it up to join the sky. Narrated stories play as each constellation twinkles in and out of view. You might even catch a meteor shower, or a shooting star streaking across the heavens.
