Desktop Cielito guide

Desktop Cielito is a small LED panel that shows all the submitted constellations. This guide covers setup and everyday use.

Setup

You need a phone/laptop and a home Wi-Fi network. The device will retrieve constellations from the internet, so it must be able to join your network. The panel does not work on mobile data or public Wi-Fi.

  1. Plug the panel into power with the USB-C cable in the box. Use a charger rated at 5V and 3A or more. The cable is for power only.
  2. On your phone, open Wi-Fi settings and join the network named Cielito Lindo Setup. The password is cielitito. You can also scan the QR code below to join the network.
  3. A setup page should open on its own. If it does not, check to see if your phone has a notification about joining the network. Tap it to open the page.
  4. Choose your home network, type its password, and save. The panel will join your network and the setup network will disappear.
  5. Make sure your phone is back on your home Wi-Fi, then scan the code on the side of the panel, or open http://cielito-lindo.local/, to see the companion page.

Changing your Wi-Fi later

If you change routers or passwords, the panel cannot join your old network. It will start Cielito Lindo Setup again. Repeat steps 2 to 4 with your new details.

The Companion Page

The companion page shows you details of the current constellation on the panel, with its name and story. It updates every few seconds. Your phone must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network as the panel.

  • Find on the Sky Map opens the full map of constellations, zoomed to the one on your panel.
  • Add a constellation opens the page where you can draw your own, name it, and tell its story. A new constellation takes about 20 minutes to reach your panel.
  • Keep this one on the sky holds the constellation you are looking at. The panel keeps it lit and pauses the rotation until you turn the switch off.
  • Device settings opens the page where you can change the panel's settings.

Anyone on your Wi-Fi network can use the companion page and change these settings.

Device settings

Open Device settings at the bottom of the companion page.

  • Family-friendly sky is on by default. It shows only constellations marked family-friendly. While it is on, you cannot hold a constellation that has not been marked family-friendly. Turn it off to see every constellation.
  • Screen dark fades the panel to black. The rotation keeps running behind it, and the panel keeps updating.
  • Dark every night turns the panel off on a daily schedule. Choose the time to turn off and the time to turn back on. Times follow your phone's time zone. This feature works in the US and Canada. If you turn the screen on or off by hand during a scheduled window, your choice holds until the next scheduled change, and then the schedule takes over again.

Troubleshooting

The companion page does not load

Confirm that your phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the panel and not on mobile data. Turn off any VPN. Try http://cielito-lindo.local/ again, or scan the code on the stand.

The panel is dark

Check that the cable is firmly seated and the charger is plugged in. Then open the companion page and check whether Screen dark or Dark every night is on.

My constellation has not appeared

Allow about 20 minutes after you submit it. The panel shows a rotating selection of the sky, so a new constellation may take some time to come around. With Family-friendly sky on, the panel skips any constellation not marked family-friendly.

The page says "Waiting for the next constellation"

The page has no constellation to show yet. This happens after the panel restarts, and after you turn on Family-friendly sky while a constellation that is not family-friendly was on screen. It fills in when the next one appears.

The panel cannot join my Wi-Fi

Check that the network is a 2.4 GHz network and that you typed the password correctly. Rejoin Cielito Lindo Setup and enter your details again.
