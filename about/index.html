About us

CoDad Labs started as a celebration of our goofy humor when we set out to build Max Loads for Burning Man 2025 (pictured below). Since then, we've had so much fun experimenting with technology as a way to create light-hearted, interactive experiences that allow people to connect with each other through play.
The name CoDad (Community Of Dreamers And Doers) is yet another silly joke — a somewhat sarcastic nod to the copy that every 501(c)(3) nonprofit seems to have to include in their mission statement.
